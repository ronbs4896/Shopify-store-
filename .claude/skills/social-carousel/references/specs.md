# Platform specs behind the carousel skill

Compiled 2026-10-09 from search-result text only (the cloud sandbox cannot open Meta, Instagram or publisher pages). "Verified" means official wording seen through a search excerpt or two or more independent sources agreeing. Nothing here was opened in a browser. Re-check before treating any row as a hard rule.

| Item | Value | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram carousel length | 2 to 20 slides (Mosseri, around August 2024; earlier step 10 to 15) | Verified |
| Feed ratios accepted | 1.91:1 to 4:5 (older Help Center text); 3:4 added May 2025 per several outlets | 4:5 verified; 3:4 contested |
| Recommended feed size | 1080x1350 | Verified |
| All slides one ratio | The first slide sets the frame, the rest are center-cropped | Verified in practice |
| Profile grid tile | 3:4 since January 2025. A 4:5 cover loses about 33 px on each side (arithmetic); a 1:1 cover loses 135 px each side | Verified (tile), derived (pixels) |
| Formats and size | JPG or PNG, sRGB, 1080 wide. A 30 MB limit is repeated but not official for organic posts | Contested |
| Caption | 2,200 characters, about 125 visible before "more" | Verified (consensus) |
| Hashtags | 5 per post since 18 or 19 December 2025 (was 30) | Verified (two outlets quoting @creators) |
| Alt text | Per slide; length limit unclear, so stay under about 100 characters | Contested |
| Stories | 1080x1920, 9:16. No organic carousel exists: post separate frames | Verified |
| Stories safe zone | Meta ad pages: 14% (250 to 270 px) top, 20% (340 to 384 px) bottom; Reels page: 14% top, 35% bottom, 6% sides. A "unified 14/35/6 zone from March 2026" appears only on third-party pages | Contested. The skill uses 270 top, 384 bottom, 92 sides |
| Facebook carousel ads | 2 to 10 cards, 1:1 at 1080x1080 is the standard, all cards one ratio, JPG or PNG up to 30 MB, minimum 600x600 | Verified |
| Facebook ad headline | 32 characters is safe (Meta pages say 40 and 45); primary text 125; description about 18 | Contested |
| Text in ad images | No limit and the overlay tool was retired; Meta's research says under 20% text performs better | Verified (official excerpt) |
| Facebook Stories | 1080x1920; 14% (250 px) free at top and bottom per Meta | Top verified, bottom contested |
| Facebook organic multi-photo | A grid collage in the feed, not a swipe carousel; use 1:1 or 4:5 and keep the first image center-safe | Unverified |
| Benchmarks | Socialinsider (2026): carousels have the highest engagement rate per post on Instagram (about 0.50% against 0.48% for Reels and 0.33% for images in Q2 2026) | One report, relayed by summaries |
| Slide count, hook, CTA | 6 to 10 slides, hook first, call to action last | Rule of thumb, no study found |

## RTL swipe test (30 minutes, two phones)

No source describes carousel behaviour in Hebrew UI. Before using swipe words or arrows:

1. Make a 4-slide test carousel at 1080x1350 with huge numbers 1 to 4. Post it to a private or test account.
2. View it on an iPhone with Hebrew UI, an Android phone with Hebrew UI, and a device with English UI.
3. Record which way the finger moves to advance, which side the next slide enters from, where the dots and the 1/4 counter sit, and which end of the dots is the first.
4. Repeat for Stories (tap right or left to go on).
5. Write the result here, then switch the cover's "החליקו להמשך" to a directional cue only if all devices agree.
